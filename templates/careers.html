ONLINE TUTORING OPPORTUNITIES · NORTH WALES TUTORING GROUP

Become an online tutor with our group.

Interested in tutoring Primary, KS3, GCSE, A-Level or entrance exam subjects online? Introduce yourself to the North Wales Tutoring Group team through Wiltshire Tutoring.

WORK WITH OUR TEAM

We welcome experienced, reliable UK-based tutors.

Our wider network includes university students with strong subject knowledge, full-time tutors, qualified teachers and examiners. Tutors work on a self-employed basis and set out the subjects, stages and times they can offer.

Tell us about your tutoring and teaching experience, your qualifications, the exam boards you know and your online availability. We particularly welcome enquiries from Maths, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, English and Welsh tutors.

Before working with students

  • Relevant subject knowledge and online teaching experience
  • Eligibility to work in the UK
  • Enhanced DBS with Children's Barred List check and safeguarding requirements
  • Professional communication through the tutoring group

Making an enquiry does not guarantee a role, allocation of students or any minimum hours. Do not send certificates or sensitive documents in this first message.

TUTOR APPLICATION ENQUIRY

Tell us about yourself.

Include your subjects, qualifications, Enhanced DBS status, proposed hourly rate and weekly availability in your message. We can request a CV separately.
