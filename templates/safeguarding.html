OUR COMMITMENT

Safeguarding is part of how we work.

Wiltshire Tutoring provides online tuition as part of North Wales Tutoring Group. We manage enquiries, tutor matching, lesson administration, payment coordination and safeguarding expectations. Tutors may work under different contractual arrangements, but our safeguarding requirements apply to tutoring delivered through our service.

1. Scope and principles

We recognise the trust placed in everyone who provides tutoring or communicates with children and young people. This policy applies to online lessons, lesson-related messages, educational materials, tutoring platforms and other contact connected with our tutoring arrangements.

Our approach is child-centred. We treat concerns seriously, respond proportionately and seek appropriate advice or referrals when needed. A parent, carer, learner, tutor or other person may report a concern without having to prove that harm has occurred.

2. Designated Safeguarding Lead

Jesse is the Founder and Designated Safeguarding Lead for the group. He receives safeguarding concerns, coordinates appropriate records and actions, and seeks advice or refers matters when required.

Dedicated safeguarding email: safeguarding@nwttutoring.com

3. Tutor vetting and suitability

We apply suitability and safeguarding checks appropriate to the role, including identity and relevant experience checks, references where appropriate and enhanced DBS arrangements for eligible tutoring roles. We require tutors to follow professional boundaries and raise concerns promptly. We review the safeguarding status of active tutors at least annually.

An enhanced DBS certificate is only one element of safer recruitment and does not, by itself, guarantee safety. Eligibility, status and any further checks are considered under applicable UK requirements.

4. Professional boundaries

Tutors must communicate appropriately, maintain a clear educational purpose, respect confidentiality and avoid secret or inappropriate communication with learners. Lessons and platforms should remain suitable for the learner's age and needs.

Parents and carers should be given appropriate information about how lessons take place and whom to contact with concerns. We do not permit tutoring-related relationships or communications that undermine safeguarding expectations.

5. Online tutoring arrangements

Online lessons should use agreed platforms and appropriate materials. Tutors must protect students' privacy, avoid unnecessary collection or sharing of personal information, and ensure that any files, messaging and screen sharing remain related to legitimate learning.

Recording sessions or sharing identifiable learner information must follow applicable law and the arrangements agreed with the family and provider. Tutors should not use private or undisclosed channels to contact children.

6. Raising and responding to concerns

Concerns can be sent to safeguarding@nwttutoring.com. Please share the facts you know, the date of the incident and an appropriate way to contact you. Avoid sending sensitive information more widely than necessary.

We assess concerns, keep appropriate records, take proportionate protective steps, and consult or refer to the appropriate statutory bodies when required. Where a concern involves a tutor, we consider steps to protect learners while the matter is managed fairly.

If someone is in immediate danger, call 999. If you have a safeguarding concern, you do not have to contact us before making a report to an appropriate authority.

7. Responding to a disclosure

Anyone who receives a disclosure from a learner should listen calmly, avoid promising secrecy, avoid leading questions and record what was said as accurately as possible. The concern must be passed promptly to the Designated Safeguarding Lead or an appropriate authority as the circumstances require.

8. Confidentiality and records

Safeguarding records are handled with appropriate care, access controls and confidentiality. Relevant information may need to be shared where lawful and necessary to protect a child or another person at risk. Privacy obligations do not prevent appropriate safeguarding action.

9. Adults at risk

Our safeguarding approach also recognises potential risks to adult learners who may need additional protection or support. Concerns are considered in line with applicable laws and guidance.

10. Review and responsibility

This policy is reviewed at least annually and updated if relevant procedures, guidance or operational arrangements change. It is intended to operate alongside the broader safeguarding framework used by North Wales Tutoring Group.
