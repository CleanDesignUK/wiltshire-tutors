ABOUT WILTSHIRE TUTORING

Online tutoring built around every learner.

Wiltshire Tutoring connects families across Wiltshire with experienced UK tutors from the North Wales Tutoring Group network. Our vision is simple: delivering a better future.

Find Your Tutor

WHO WE ARE

One-to-one support from a wider UK tutoring network.

We're a Wiltshire-focused branch of North Wales Tutoring Group, which supports learners across the UK. This means families in Swindon, Salisbury, Trowbridge, Chippenham and smaller Wiltshire communities can ask us about a broad range of subjects and qualifications.

We focus on finding the right fit: a tutor who understands your subject, current ability, learning goals and exam board. Tuition is delivered online, so there is no need to travel to a centre.

We aim to make the process clear, supportive and straightforward, from the first enquiry through ongoing lessons.

WHAT MATTERS TO US

Good tutoring starts with good listening.

Clarity

Breaking down difficult concepts into ideas students can explain and practise themselves.

Care

Respecting the learner’s confidence, pace and individual needs throughout tuition.

Progress

Using feedback and practice to make the next steps specific and achievable.

Safeguarding

Following our safeguarding procedures, vetting expectations and professional boundaries.
