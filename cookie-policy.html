Cookie Policy

A clear explanation of the storage and external services used on this website.

Essential website functionality

This website uses localStorage under the key wiltshire_tutoring_cookie_choice_v1 to remember the visitor’s response to the cookie banner. This preference can be cleared in your browser settings.

The site may also use technical storage necessary for delivery of the website, its security and normal browser operation.

Optional analytics and advertising

No Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, advertising pixel or other optional audience-tracking scripts are installed in this launch version. Accepting the banner does not activate any analytics service. We would update this notice and ask for appropriate consent before adding optional tracking.

Third-party resources

The website loads Google Fonts and SweetAlert scripts from external providers. Our Bootstrap CSS/JavaScript and icon assets are hosted on this website. Our enquiry forms send information to Web3Forms only when you choose to submit them.

External services have their own privacy practices and may use technical cookies or logs necessary to provide their service.

Changing your preferences

To reset the cookie banner, clear site data or localStorage for wiltshiretutors.com in your browser. You may also block optional cookies using your browser controls.

Questions

For cookie or privacy questions, contact wiltshire@nwttutoring.com.
